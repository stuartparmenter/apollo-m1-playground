Flash your HUB75 matrix from the browser

Plug your controller in over USB, pick it below, and you will be running HUB75 Studio in a couple of minutes. No toolchain, no command line.

1 Choose your controller

Controller

Not sure which M-1 you have? The revision is printed on the back of the board.

2 Connect and install

Connect the controller to this computer with a USB data cable, then click Connect and pick its serial port. Installing erases whatever is on the device and writes a fresh copy of HUB75 Studio.

Your browser cannot talk to serial devices. Use desktop Chrome or Edge, or flash the firmware from Releases with web.esphome.io. Serial access needs a secure page. Open this installer over HTTPS.

During install you can join the device to Wi-Fi. If you skip that, it opens its own HUB75 Studio hotspot you can connect to afterwards to set the network.

3 Adopt it in Home Assistant

Once the device is on your Wi-Fi it announces itself on the network. In Home Assistant go to Settings → Devices & services, where it shows up as a discovered ESPHome device. Click Configure and you are done.

Want to customize the firmware?

The firmware ships with dashboard_import, so the device also appears in the ESPHome Dashboard (or the ESPHome add-on) under Discovered. Click Take control to pull its configuration in, then edit the YAML and flash updates over Wi-Fi. Panel size, weather entities, presence entities, DDP streaming and WizMote pairing all live there. The customization guide walks through the common changes.

What you get

Every install includes the full set of pages. Turn them on and off, and run more than one copy of a page, from Home Assistant.

  • Now PlayingAlbum art, track info and a progress bar, per media player.
  • Team TrackerLive scores and game state for the teams you follow.
  • Clock & WeatherTime, forecast, person status and countdown timers.
  • Audio SpectrumReal-time visualizer on controllers with a microphone.
  • Visual EffectsFireworks, fireplace and aurora, plus a physics sandbox.
  • PongPlayable on the panel, controlled from Home Assistant.
  • MSR-2 RadarPlots mmWave presence targets as they move around the room.
  • QR & DDPShow a QR code, or stream pixels to the panel over DDP.
